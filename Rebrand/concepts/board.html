a quieter m.

Four directions, one idea each — no gradients, no mascots, no cleverness for its own sake. Every mark is drawn geometry (circles and lines, round terminals), sized to survive a 16-px tray icon and an iOS squircle. The app is calm; the logo should be too.

01

arch m

the quiet default

A monoline lowercase m: three stems, two perfect semicircles, every terminal rounded. No trick — the brand is the restraint. It matches Outfit's geometry, so mark and wordmark feel cut from the same metal.

watch: it's so quiet it leans on the wordmark for personality.
