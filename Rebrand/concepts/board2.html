the dot

color · position · weight

The dot is the only voice the brand has, so it earns a study. Color: bone is quietest, red is yours — the app's dark-mode accent. Blue belongs to light-mode UI, not the brand. Position: the baseline period is the meaning ("done"); raised dots read as math or punctuation tricks. Weight: the display cut keeps the dot honest at icon sizes.

call: red dot, baseline, both weights mastered.

C

the fusion — killed

shown for the record

The idea was one glyph in both scripts: the m's last stem ending in the meem's tail. Two cuts were drawn; both read as "mj", not calligraphy. The meem's tail works because it falls out of a round head — on a straight stem it's a typo. Killed rather than polished.

but it pointed somewhere: the heritage carrier was already in the system. Arabic builds its alphabet with dots — a dot completing a letter is the Arabic gesture. m. was bilingual before we tried to force it.
